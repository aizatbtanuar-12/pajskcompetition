Three rules to know first

These decide which competitions are worth the fee.

Y4–Y6

Only these years count

The primary PAJSK score is the average of Years 4, 5 and 6. Entries in Years 1–3 are good practice but earn no marks.

Yearly

Approval is per edition

KPM approves a competition's PAJSK status each year, usually after the event. Recognised last year is a good sign, not a promise. Keep every certificate.

Medals

Gold ≠ extra marks

Gold, Silver and Bronze awards in STEM and arts contests don't count as a placing. The marks come from the participation level: national or international.
